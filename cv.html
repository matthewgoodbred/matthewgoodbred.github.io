Research experience

Graduate Research, Princeton University Sept 2024 – present
Advisor: Anatoly Spitkovsky
  • Ran 3D particle-in-cell simulations of pulsar magnetospheres on HPC clusters, resolving the disk–dome equilibrium and the onset of the diocotron instability.
  • Showed the nonlinear diocotron state is a persistent m = 1 mode; derived a quasilinear diffusion coefficient for cross-field charge transport through the closed zone.
  • Linked the instability to emission-beam perturbations and to observed nulling, periodic amplitude modulation, and drifting subpulses.
Magnetic Reconnection Group, Dartmouth College June 2020 – June 2023
Advisor: Yi-Hsin Liu
  • Derived the first analytic theory of relativistic reconnection rates; confirmed with PIC simulations.
  • Related energy conversion and reconnection rates in Petschek reconnection; applied to solar flares.
