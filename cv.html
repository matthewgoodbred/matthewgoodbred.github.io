Research experience

Graduate Research, Princeton University Sept 2023 – present
Advisor: Anatoly Spitkovsky
  • 3D particle-in-cell simulations (Tristan-MP v2) of aligned and oblique pulsar magnetospheres on national and institutional HPC resources, resolving the disk-dome equilibrium and the onset of the diocotron instability.
  • Established that the nonlinear diocotron state is a long-lived m = 1 mode carrying a few percent of the central point charge, and derived a quasilinear radial diffusion coefficient for stochastic cross-field charge transport through the closed zone.
  • Developed the multipole framework relating diocotron charge moments to polar-cap potential and emission-beam perturbations, connecting the instability to nulling, periodic amplitude modulation, and drifting subpulses.
  • Wrote and maintain Python analysis and visualization pipelines for large 3D PIC datasets, plus an independent N-body electrosphere code for equilibrium studies.
Magnetic Reconnection Group, Dartmouth College June 2020 – June 2023
Advisor: Yi-Hsin Liu
  • Developed the first analytic theory for the rate of relativistic magnetic reconnection in magnetically dominated pair plasmas; validated predictions against large-scale PIC simulations (first-author PRL).
  • Derived a theory relating energy conversion rate to reconnection rate in Petschek-type reconnection, with application to solar flares (first-author Physics of Plasmas).
Quantum Engineering Group, Dartmouth College Sept 2022 – Nov 2022
Advisor: Sekhar Ramanathan
  • Extended an AlphaZero-based reinforcement learning algorithm to design radio pulse sequences for quantum control, producing sequences outperforming the state of the art.
